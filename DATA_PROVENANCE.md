# Data Provenance, Linguistic Corpora & Font Licensing

This document provides a transparent, verifiable record of the provenance, licensing, and usage rights for all linguistic corpora, digital typefaces, and synthetic benchmarks utilized in the **Cham-OCR** project.

## 1. Primary Text Corpus (`cham_text.txt`)

* **File Location**: `ocr-training/data/corpus/cham_text.txt` (approx. 733 KB, 18,829 textlines)
* **Script Tradition**: Eastern Cham (*Akhar Thrah*, Unicode Block `U+AA00`–`U+AA5F`)
* **Linguistic Content**:
  - Excerpts from classical Cham epics and poetic narrative traditions, including *Akayet Inra Patra* and *Ariya Po Pareng*.
  - Historical chronicles, folk tales, and ceremonial liturgical phrases.
  - Cham-Vietnamese and Cham-French vocabulary compendia and grammatical paradigms.
* **Corpus Curation & Preprocessing**:
  - Normalization into standard Unicode 16.0 Cham representation (U+AA00–U+AA5F).
  - Removal of incompatible characters, unmapped symbols, and malformed encoding artifacts.
  - Segment-level line breaking tailored for textline sequence recognition.
* **Legal, Cultural & Research Usage Status**:
  - Classical Cham literature belongs to the collective cultural heritage of the Cham people. Specific underlying manuscript rights remain unrecorded and traditional.
  - The curated text corpus (`cham_text.txt`) is compiled and distributed solely for non-commercial linguistic preservation, educational scholarship, and machine learning research under fair use / non-commercial research principles. We make no proprietary claims over the underlying cultural literature, nor do we assert undocumented open-license grants (such as CC BY) over texts of traditional provenance.

## 3. Synthetic Document & Textline Datasets

Due to the absence of large-scale, character-level annotated historical Cham manuscript datasets, training and benchmark datasets were synthesized programmatically:

1. **150,000 Textline Synthesis Dataset** (`scripts/generate_data.py`):
   - Combines sentences from `cham_text.txt` with the validated font suite.
   - Structured into 4 stratified tiers: Classical Literature (50%), Multilingual Code-Switching (18%), Verse Numerals & Section Marks 1–99 (18%), and Adversarial Diacritic Minimal Pairs (14%).
   - Augmented on-the-fly with Point Spread Function (PSF) motion blur, paper grain, non-linear lighting gradients, and spatial elastic deformation.
2. **Controlled 200-Page Synthetic Document Stress-Test** (`ocr-benchmark/`):
   - 200 full-page synthetic documents comprising 903 ground-truth textlines across 5 difficulty levels (Standard, Aged Paper, Narrow Gap, Wavy Sinusoid, Extreme Overlap).
   - Generated by `ocr-benchmark/scripts/generate_benchmark_200.py` to establish the mathematical breaking point of line segmentation and CTC recognition.
3. **50-Test Stratified Diagnostic Suite** (`ocr-studio/data/benchmark_50_tests_results.json`):
   - 50 targeted synthetic image crops covering 10 distinct failure modes (noise, tilt, blur, diacritic confusion, low resolution).
